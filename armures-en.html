Avenue des Armures — Forest

A magnificent house transformed into three high-end homes, a stone's throw from Altitude Cent.

Completed project — Sold

A house of character, three exceptional homes

At 50 avenue des Armures, on a quiet one-way street facing the Altitude Cent square, Broperties carried out the complete, high-end renovation of a beautiful house with a 7.5-metre façade. It now holds three homes: a ground-floor apartment opening onto a landscaped garden, a two-bedroom apartment on the first floor, and a three-bedroom duplex on the second and third floors.

  • Complete renovation: electricity, plumbing, boilers, double glazing
  • Oak floors · high ceilings
  • Fully equipped bespoke kitchens
  • Bathrooms with bathtub and walk-in shower
  • Acoustic insulation of ceilings and floors
  • Large private cellars
  • Landscaped garden and terraces
