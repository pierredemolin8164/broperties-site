From garage to four duplexes — Schaerbeek

Rue Emile Wittmann 34 · In preparation

Upcoming project — In preparation

Four duplexes around a garden

At 34 rue Emile Wittmann in Schaerbeek, Broperties is preparing the conversion of a former garage into four duplexes: two on the street side, two to the rear, with a green space reintroduced between the two buildings. A wooden street façade, green roof, planted patio and interiors in natural tones designed with an interior architecture studio — more information soon.

  • Green space reintroduced between the two buildings

  • Planted patio and bicycle storage
