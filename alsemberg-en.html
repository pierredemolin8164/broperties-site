Chaussée d'Alsemberg — Uccle

A shop and three high-end homes, in the heart of the Parvis Saint-Pierre district.

Completed project — Sold

A building, entirely reinvented

At 710-712 chaussée d'Alsemberg, in the heart of Uccle, Broperties converted and extended an existing building into a new, charge-free building: a shop on the ground floor, a magnificent four-bedroom 230 m² duplex opening onto its private garden, and two two-bedroom apartments upstairs, beneath the green roofs. An interior architect oversaw the entire project.

  • Open living spaces, bathed in light
  • Fully equipped open-plan kitchens
  • Bathrooms with bathtub and walk-in shower
  • High-end materials and finishes · Italian tiling
  • Video intercom · remotely controlled thermostat · armoured door
  • Rainwater tank with pump · shared bicycle storage
  • Green roofs
